# Cooperative Economic Model & Social Security Framework
## SahakarSeva: Empowering Labour Cooperative Federations
**Client Authority:** Ministry of Cooperation | National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT)  
**Problem Statement ID:** 26089  

---

## 1. Context & The Need for a Cooperative Alternative

### 1.1 The Private Aggregator Crisis
Modern gig aggregators (e.g., Urban Company, TaskRabbit, Swiggy/Zomato) operate on shareholder-maximization models resulting in:
- **Exorbitant Commission Rates:** 20% to 35% commission cut per gig.
- **De-humanizing Algorithmic Punishments:** Workers are blocked or de-ranked for declining gigs or taking sick days.
- **Zero Social Safety Net:** Workers are classified as "independent contractors" with no health insurance, provident fund, or accident cover.
- **Worker Debt Traps:** Workers are forced to purchase branded uniforms and costly kits from the private platform.

### 1.2 The Cooperative Solution
India has over **30,000 Labour Cooperative Societies** with millions of skilled blue-collar workers. **SahakarSeva** brings digital parity to these societies, turning workers into **co-owners** of the platform through the cooperative federation.

## 3. Social Security & Micro-Insurance Integration

The 5% welfare deduction accumulates automatically in the worker's digital welfare ledger:

### 3.1 Government Scheme Auto-Enrolment
1. **Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY):**
   - Annual premium: ₹20/year.
   - Accidental death/disability coverage: ₹2,00,000.
   - Trigger: As soon as a worker completes their first gig and accumulates ₹20 in the welfare bucket, the platform auto-renews the premium via partner cooperative banks.
2. **Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY):**
   - Annual premium: ₹436/year.
   - Term life coverage: ₹2,00,000.
   - Trigger: Funded automatically once the welfare pool hits the threshold.
3. **Ayushman Bharat / Micro-Health Shield:**
   - Subsidized bi-annual health screenings at cooperative district hospitals.

### 3.2 Tool & Equipment Rental Subsidies
Primary societies use their 10% operational fund to purchase high-grade industrial tools (e.g., rotary hammer drills, pipe crimping tools, thermal cameras). Workers rent these tools at nominal daily fees, eliminating personal debt.

## 4. NCCT Skill Profiling & Career Progression Ladder

The **National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT)** provides a formal multi-tier skill certification framework embedded within SahakarSeva:

```
  [ Level 1: Sahakari Shikshu (Apprentice) ]
     │  - Basic skill assessment + soft-skills & safety training
     │  - Paired with Master Craftsman for first 30 gigs
     ▼
  [ Level 2: Sahakari Karigar (Certified Craftsman) ]
     │  - Verified 100+ gigs with rating ≥ 4.3
     │  - Standard rate authorization + independent dispatch
     ▼
  [ Level 3: Sahakari Ustad (Master Craftsman / Trainer) ]
     │  - NCCT Master Certified (Industrial/Commercial grades)
     │  - Eligible for complex institutional contracts (RWAs, Govt. complexes)
     │  - Receives mentor stipend for training Level 1 apprentices
```

## 5. Patronage Dividend Distribution Mechanism

At the end of each financial year:
1. Surplus operating funds within the Cooperative Federation are calculated.
2. Under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 50% of the net surplus is redistributed back to worker-members as **Patronage Dividends**, directly proportional to the total number of hours and gigs completed on the platform.
3. This creates unmatched long-term worker loyalty and community goodwill.
